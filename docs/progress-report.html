URL Shortener - Progress Report

Updated 12 Nov 2026, ~12:29 PM IST. Code: github.com/Shubh12970/url-shortener (private). Live demo: https://url-shortener.2wf0w2.workers.dev

Where the project stands

The URL shortener is live and review-ready. You can paste a long link, get a short one, share it, and watch the click count. Links can have an optional expiry date and a QR code. Everything runs free: Cloudflare Workers + D1, no credit card.

Owner accounts are live (stage 2): sign in to keep links on your account, see private stats, and delete links. Anonymous use still works as before - "Your links" is browser-local, anonymous links keep public stats, and they can never be deleted or reassigned. Details and honest limitations are in the Documentation doc.

11 Nov 2026 additions, live and tested, pending your review: an 'Unguessable code' option at creation (random 8-character codes that cannot be found by walking sequential numbers) and a clicks-by-hour heat strip on the stats view (24 UTC buckets, plain CSS). 11 Nov 2026 - distinctive additions (milestone 5), ~10:50 PM IST

Unguessable codes, opt-in: an 'Unguessable code' checkbox at creation asks for a random 8-character base62 code drawn from crypto randomness (the operating system's secure random source). Bytes that would slightly favour some characters are discarded (rejection sampling), so every character is equally likely; if a random code ever collided, the insert would retry with a fresh draw. Sequential auto codes stay the default; custom codes are unchanged.

Clicks by hour: the stats endpoint now also groups clicks by UTC hour of day (24 buckets) and the stats view draws them as a plain-CSS heat strip, brighter where more clicks landed.

3 new unit tests (31/31 pass). Verified live: a random code was created and redirected, the by-hour strip lit the correct UTC hour, and sequential and custom codes behaved as before.

Known limits (the honest version)

  • Auto codes are sequential base62 by default, so they are enumerable - someone could walk codes and find links. The 'Unguessable code' option at creation (11 Nov 2026) avoids this for new links. Stats for anonymous links (everything created before accounts) stay public to anyone with the code; owned links have private stats.
  • Click counts are approximate: bots and link prefetchers trigger redirects, and a failed async count write loses one click.
  • Cached redirects can keep working up to 60 seconds after a change or deletion; expiring links skip the cache for that reason.
  • Timestamps are UTC, so the per-day chart rolls over at 5:30 AM IST, not midnight.
  • D1 (the database) is a single-region SQLite: free tier 10 databases, 500 MB each, 5 GB total; the Workers free plan allows 100,000 requests/day (official limits pages, checked 12 Nov 2026). Generous for a learning project, not unlimited.
  • Sign-in runs on Clerk's free development instance: capped at 100 users with a "Development mode" label. A production instance needs a domain we control - fine for a learning demo.
